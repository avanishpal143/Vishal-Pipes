RDSO APPROVED RAILWAY & HIGHWAY STRUCTURES

HEAVY STEEL FABRICATION

Precision structural steel fabrication of open web bridge girders, plate girders, and box girders engineered for demanding railway and highway bridge applications.

HEAVY GIRDER CAPABILITIES

LARGE-SCALE GIRDER FABRICATION. TESTED INTEGRITY.

After completion of the automated Submerged Arc Welding (SAW) fabrication process, girders are thoroughly shot blasted to Sa 2.5 cleanliness, fitted with shear connector studs, and efficiently metallized or painted with epoxy primer systems.

Our fabrication bays can handle flanges from 140mm to 1200mm (thickness 6mm to 80mm) and web plates from 180mm to 3000mm (thickness 6mm to 60mm) with continuous piece lengths up to 20 meters, alongside customized sizes tailored to EPC tender designs.

Approved by RDSO (Ministry of Railways, Govt of India)

Submit Fabrication Drawings →
TYPE // 01

Plate Girders

Built-up I-sections engineered with top/bottom flange plates and web plates. High capacity to carry load in shear, tension, and bending compression across highway flyovers and industrial cranes.

TYPE // 02

Open Web Railway Girders

Used for railway track bridges crossing wide rivers and deep valleys. Standard Indian Railways spans manufactured include 30.5m, 45.7m, 61.0m, and 76.2 meters.

TYPE // 03

Box Girders

Single and multi-cell hollow box cross-sections delivering extreme torsional stiffness and resistance to warping, ideally suited for horizontally curved flyovers and elevated metro viaducts.
