ESTABLISHED IN 1988

40 YEARS OF MANUFACTURING EXCELLENCE.

Behold the world around you. Virtually all major infrastructure—skyscrapers, industrial complexes, bridges, railways, energy substations, irrigation networks, and gas pipelines—rely on precision pipes, tubes, and engineered steel sections.

Headquartered in New Delhi with integrated manufacturing complexes spanning over 150,000 square meters at Sikandrabad (Greater Noida Part-II), Uttar Pradesh, Vishal Pipes Limited (Brand VPL India) manufactures products certified to national and international standards. Equipped with state-of-the-art PLC-controlled rolling mills, automated galvanizing baths, CNC plasma cutters, and dedicated metallurgical testing laboratories, we engineer solutions built to withstand the harshest operating stresses.

QUESTIONS & SPECIFICATIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Technical clarity on manufacturing capacities, standard sizes, custom fabrication, quality certifications, and international logistics.

Vishal Pipes manufactures ERW Black and Galvanized steel pipes conforming to IS 1239, IS 3589, IS 1161, and international ASTM/BS standards. Diameters range from 15mm (1/2") to 350mm NB in circular hollow sections, alongside square and rectangular structural hollow sections with comprehensive hydrostatic and eddy-current testing.
We are certified under ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, and CE marking. Furthermore, we hold official vendor approvals from RDSO (Research Designs and Standards Organisation, Ministry of Railways) for bridge girders and Engineers India Limited (EIL) for IS 3589 ERW pipes, alongside state electricity boards, power corporations, and CPWD.
Yes. Our heavy steel fabrication facility in Sikandrabad fabricates plate girders, open web railway bridge girders (30.5m to 76.2m standard railway lengths), box girders, and substation gantries up to 765 KV. Fabrication includes full in-house automated SAW welding, shear connector studs, shot blasting to Sa 2.5, and zinc metalizing/epoxy painting.
Our polymer division manufactures HDPE pipes ranging from outer diameter (OD) 20mm to 400mm with pressure classes from PN 2.5 to PN 20 (IS 4984 compliant). We also manufacture UPVC plumbing/pressure pipes, CPVC hot/cold distribution pipes, and MDPE pipes for city gas networks.
We export across the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and Asia. Pipes and poles are prepared with hex-bundling, end-protection caps, seaworthy plastic wrapping, and color-coded identification. All documentation (MTC, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, inspection reports) is managed by our dedicated international trade division.
